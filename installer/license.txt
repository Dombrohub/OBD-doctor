OBD EFT - Car Diagnostics
Version 1.11.0

IMPORTANT - PLEASE READ

OBD EFT reads data from your vehicle's on-board diagnostics (OBD-II) system through an ELM327-compatible adapter and suggests likely causes for problems.

1. GUIDANCE ONLY. The diagnosis is based on trouble codes and sensor data and is provided as guidance. It is not a substitute for inspection by a qualified mechanic. Always have safety-related problems (brakes, steering, airbags, overheating, low oil pressure) checked by a professional.

2. ABS AND AIRBAG SYSTEMS. Faults in these systems affect your safety. Have them repaired by a qualified professional. Clearing an airbag or ABS warning does not repair the fault, and while an airbag fault is present the airbags may not deploy.

3. CLEARING CODES. Clearing trouble codes turns off the check engine light but does not repair the fault, erases freeze-frame data and resets emissions readiness monitors. Only clear codes with the engine off and the ignition on.

4. AI EXPLANATIONS. The optional AI feature sends the trouble codes, sensor readings and any vehicle description or symptoms you type (but not your VIN) to Anthropic's Claude API, using your own API key and subject to Anthropic's terms. AI explanations can be wrong; verify them before buying parts or making repairs.

5. DRIVING. Do not operate the software while driving. Have a passenger use it or park the vehicle first.
